How to use these tests

1. Do it on paper first

Write your real answer out. Don't just think "I know this" — thinking it and writing it are very different.

2. Then check the marks

Every question shows you where each mark comes from. Compare it to what you wrote, line by line.

3. Write down what you missed

Keep one notebook of mistakes. Before the exam, that notebook is worth more than your textbook.
