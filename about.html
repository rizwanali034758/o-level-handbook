Why this exists

Most O Level students in Pakistan have the same three problems, and none of them is a shortage of information.

😵 Too much, badly organised

Thousands of past papers exist online. Almost none of it tells you which question to do, when, or why you lost the mark.

🗣️ Everything is an English exam

Marks are awarded for precise English sentences — even in Pakistan Studies and Islamiyat. Nobody teaches this directly.

📉 Studying without feedback

Reading a chapter feels productive and often teaches nothing. Without marking and correction, hours disappear with no gain.

This site was built to attack exactly those three things.

What makes it different

Not the amount of content. Bigger sites exist. The difference is what we did with the papers.

📖 We actually read the papers

Every Physics Paper 2 from 2023 to 2026 — both variants, all 14 papers — read question by question. Then indexed by topic, marks and command word, with a note on what each one is really testing.

🎯 We tell you where marks are lost

Not "revise electricity." Instead: two 1.5V cells in parallel still give 1.5V, not 3V — the specific mistake, on the specific question, that costs real students real marks.

🧭 We measure before we advise

The Starting Point Check tests vocabulary, grammar, reading and exam command words separately, then builds a plan around your actual weak spots — not a generic one.

✅ Progress must be earned

Ticking a checkbox proves nothing. You can tick everything and still get a D. Real progress comes from answering questions, so that is what we count.

How it is built

  • Straight from the official syllabus. Every topic and sub-topic comes from the Cambridge syllabus document for that exact exam year — not a textbook's version of it.
  • Syllabus year and qualification checked every time. A subject with a similar name can be a completely different qualification. Urdu 0539 is IGCSE, not O Level. ICT 0417 is not Computer Science 2210. We verify both, because getting this wrong wastes months.
  • Real questions referenced, never copied. Paper, question number, marks and command word — plus our own description. You get the genuine paper and official mark scheme from the source.
  • Nothing invented. If we have not read a paper, we say so. We do not make up question numbers or statistics.

What it costs

Nothing, and that is not a trial No free tier, no premium version, no account, no email address, no ads, no tracking. Your progress is stored on your own device and we never see it. If that ever changes, we will say so clearly on this page first.

Being honest about limits

A few things we would rather say out loud:

  • This is a study aid, not a teacher. It does not replace a good school or a good tutor.
  • Subject coverage is uneven. Physics is deeply indexed; other subjects are still being built. We show you which is which rather than pretending otherwise.
  • We are new. There will be mistakes. Tell us and we will fix them — accuracy matters more to us than appearing finished.
