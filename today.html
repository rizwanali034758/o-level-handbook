Today

Three things. About 25 minutes. That's the whole job.

🎉 Done for today That's the streak kept alive. Doing this again tomorrow matters more than doing extra today.

Why only three things?

🧠 Small beats heroic

Studying 25 minutes every day beats 4 hours once a week. Your brain needs repeated visits, not one long one.

📖 English runs daily

Every subject is marked on precise English sentences — even Pakistan Studies and Islamiyat. Ten minutes a day compounds over years.

🔁 Old topics come back

You forget most of what you learn within weeks unless you revisit it. That's normal, and the fix is revisiting on schedule.
