Three mistakes that cost the most marks

❌ Studying without the syllabus

You end up learning things that cannot be asked, and missing things that will be. Download it first. It takes two minutes.

❌ Saving past papers for the end

Past papers are not a final test. They are how you learn what the questions look like. Start them as soon as you finish a topic.

❌ Counting hours instead of work

Six distracted hours beat nothing, but three focused hours beat six distracted ones. Ask what you finished, not how long you sat.
