Starting Point Check

This is not a test you can fail. There is no grade and no score to be embarrassed about.

It finds out what you already know, so your study plan is built for you — not for an imaginary average student.

⏱️ 15–30 minutes

English takes 10–20 minutes depending on reading speed. Urdu takes about 10. You can do them separately.

🤝 Answer honestly

Guessing to look good makes the plan worse. Wrong answers are the useful ones — they show us what to teach.

🚫 No help

Do it alone. No dictionary, no phone, no one sitting next to you. A wrong result helps nobody.

For the parent or guardian Let her do this alone and do not watch over her shoulder. If she feels judged, she will guess to look good, and the plan we build will target the wrong things. The results are only useful if they are real.

How would you like to read the Urdu?

Both are fine. Choose whichever feels easier — this itself tells us something useful.
